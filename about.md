I'm Oscar Oluoch — a software engineer who cares about getting things right.

I like working at the intersection of problem and craft: understanding a system well enough to know exactly where to press, then building something that holds. Most of what I do involves backend systems, distributed infrastructure, and the kind of software that runs quietly and reliably in the background.

I'm drawn to problems with real constraints — the ones where the naive solution obviously won't work and you have to think harder. I read widely: systems papers, field notes from practitioners, the occasional philosophy of engineering.

This blog is where I think out loud. Notes on things I've built, read, or figured out. Some of it is technical. Some of it is about the practice of building software as a craft.
